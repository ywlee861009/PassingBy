Original maps are based on NASA imagery and elevation data; incomplete areas may be artistically reconstructed. Colors in that pack are adjusted for visualization.

Modifications by Passing By: resized/reprojected to shaded spherical views, composited Earth clouds, added lighting, gamma correction, limb shading and alpha edges. Saturn's rings and the launch landscape are code-generated illustrations. These views are visualizations, not live observations or data for scientific measurements.

Unresolved exoplanet surfaces are original procedural interpretations, labeled as such in the course notes. No exoplanet surface image is represented as an observation.
